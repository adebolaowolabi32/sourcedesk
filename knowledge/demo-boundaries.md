# What this demo can and cannot do

## Synthetic payments only

LedgerDesk uses fictional companies, recipients, and transfers. No real money moves. It does not connect to a bank, issue refunds, calculate fees or tax, or promise real-world settlement times.

## Support assistant scope

SourceDesk explains the published synthetic support guides. It cannot inspect your bank, retrieve live balances, approve a payment, or change credentials. Account-specific payment status, disputes, missing funds, and undocumented policies require human review.
