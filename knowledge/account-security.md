# Passwords and account recovery

## Changing your password

Use Account to enter your current password and a new password of 12 to 200 characters. A password change signs out every session and invalidates older unused recovery links. Sign in again with the new password.

## Forgotten passwords

Choose Forgot password on the sign-in screen and enter your email. If the account exists, recovery instructions are queued for the configured delivery adapter. Recovery tokens expire after 30 minutes and can be used once. Never share your password or recovery token with support.

## Joining from an invitation

An administrator can invite a member with an email and a role. Invitation tokens expire after 24 hours. Use Accept invitation on the sign-in screen. Existing account holders must use their current password; new accounts need a password of at least 12 characters.
