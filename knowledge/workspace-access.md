# Roles and workspace access

## Available roles

Makers create payment requests. Checkers approve or reject independent requests and retry exhausted failures. Administrators manage members, workspaces, and simulator settings. Viewers can read records but cannot create or review payments.

## Switching workspaces

Use the workspace selector to choose a workspace where you have an active membership. Payments, beneficiaries, and history are scoped to that workspace. A member of Cedar Finance cannot read Harbor Labs records without a Harbor Labs membership.

## Membership changes

An administrator can change a member role or deactivate membership from Account. Changes apply to subsequent requests, including existing sessions. At least one active administrator must remain in each workspace.
