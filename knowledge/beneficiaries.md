# Recipients and payment amounts

## Supported currency and amount

LedgerDesk accepts Nigerian naira amounts from NGN 0.01 to NGN 99,999,999.99 with at most two decimal places. The API accepts a decimal string and stores integer kobo. Other currencies are not supported in this demo.

## Selecting a recipient

Choose a saved beneficiary in your active workspace and verify the displayed bank and account suffix before submitting. The demo displays only the last four account digits. It does not verify real bank account ownership or support live bank transfers.
