# Independent approvals

## Who can approve a payment

A checker or administrator can approve a pending payment only if someone else created it. Makers and viewers cannot approve. Administrators cannot approve their own requests; ask another checker.

## Rejecting an incorrect request

A reviewer can reject a pending request with a reason of 5 to 300 characters. Rejected payments are not sent to the provider. A corrected request needs a new payment and another independent review.

## The request changed during review

A stale version error means the payment changed after you opened it. Refresh Payment details and read the latest status before reviewing. Approval is single-use; do not repeatedly submit the old review.
